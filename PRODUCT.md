# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Customers browsing and ordering perfumes, often on a phone, and shop staff managing stock, orders, delivery, and support.

## Product Purpose
Help customers choose a perfume by scent notes, inspiration, or collection, then place and track a cash-on-delivery order.

## Operating Context
Customers can check out as guests or link a customer account. Staff use a separate dashboard. The store provides phone and text support at 09243483889.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The catalog uses the shop’s original bottle photos. Current prices and availability come from inventory. Checkout handles delivery fees, stock reservation, and order confirmations. Orders can be tracked by reference; linked accounts also offer private order history.

## Brand Commitments
The Vinity is the storefront name. Pink and purple are the requested colors. Preserve the pictured products and the information supplied by the shop.

## Product Principles
- Make the catalog and checkout easy to use on a narrow phone screen.
- Keep price, availability, and order status clear.
- Keep account and staff information scoped to the people entitled to see it.
