Frequently asked questions

Where can I see prices?

Each perfume shows its current price in the collection. Review your subtotal and delivery fee at checkout.

How do I place an order?

Add your fragrances to the bag, enter your delivery details at checkout, and submit your order.

Where can I see my order status?

Use your order reference under Track order. Orders placed with a linked account also appear in your account.
